# Privacy Policy — Verse Bible

_Last updated: 2025_

Verse Bible ("the app") is a Bible reader and verse-wallpaper generator.
We designed it to work **entirely on your device**.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no
crash reporting. We do not receive, store, or sell any personal data.

## Data stored on your device
- Bible text (bundled, public-domain translations).
- Your highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading position and settings.
- Wallpaper images (bundled seed images and, if enabled, downloaded photos).
- A short history of automatically applied wallpapers.

All of this stays in the app's private storage. You can export it as a backup
file from **Settings → Your data**, and it is removed when you uninstall.

## Network access
The app only uses the network if **you enable "Download new photos"** in
Wallpaper → Automation. It then requests photos from
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/privacy) and/or [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/)
using a search term you choose (e.g. "nature"). Those requests contain no
personal identifiers beyond what any HTTP request carries (your IP address).
Photographer attribution is displayed as required by those services.

## Permissions
- **Set wallpaper** (Android) — to apply the generated image.
- **Notifications** — optional Verse of the Day reminder.
- **Photos – add only** (iOS) — to save wallpapers you create.
- **Background tasks** — to rotate wallpapers on your chosen schedule.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Updates to this policy will ship with the app and be listed here.
